Questions

Good to know.

What if my photos aren't great?

Most listings have 20+ professional photos, which is plenty. We pick the strongest 8 to 12. If the set is thin, we tell you before you pay.

Can I use the reel on Instagram and in ads?

Yes. The reel is yours to post anywhere, and the music is licensed for social use.

Do I need to own the photos?

Use photos you have the right to market the listing with, which normally covers the photos on your own listing.

What if I don't like it?

One revision is included, and we keep going until the reel is right for your listing.
